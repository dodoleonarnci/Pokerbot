    How the table was built

    ~/work/dr_offline/build_preflop.py deals each of the 1,755 distinct three-card hands 5,000 times against one random three-card hand, and 2,500 times against a hand from the top 20%. On the flop both players keep whichever two cards make the stronger hand right then, the board runs out, and the better five-card hand wins. Nobody bets or folds, and the exposed card is ignored.

    Strength percentile ranks hands by equity against a random hand, weighted by how many card combinations each one has (22,100 in all). With 5,000 deals the standard error of an equity is about 0.7 points (1 point against the top 20%), so nearby hands that differ by less than that are tied.
